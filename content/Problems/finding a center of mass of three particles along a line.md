## assignment
- Three objects are located along the $x$-axis, shown on the image.![[Pasted image 20260224161200.png]]
- Determine the [[center of mass|CoM]] of this system of objects.

## solution
- ### step 1
	- In such problems we have to approach the solution mathematically. Lets recall the formula for the [[center of mass|CoM]].
	- $$x_{cm}=\frac{{\sum m_{i}x_{i}}}{\sum m_{i}}$$
	- Now let's apply it to our system:
	- $$x_{cm}=\frac{{(3\,\text{kg})(0\,\text{m}) + (5\,\text{kg})(3\,\text{m}) + (2\,\text{kg})(5\,\text{m})}}{(3\,\text{kg}+5\,\text{kg}+2\,\text{kg})} = 2.5\,\text{m}$$
