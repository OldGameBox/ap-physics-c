## assignment
- Given a position function, $x(t)=2t^{2}+3$ ...
- ### part 1
	- Find the average velocity between $t=0\,\text{s}$ and $t=4\,\text{s}$.
- ### part 2
	- Find the average velocity between $t=3\,\text{s}$ and $t=0.5\,\text{s}$.

## solution
- ### part 1
	- $$v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{{x(4)-x(0)}}{4-0}=\frac{{35-3}}{4}=8 \frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$$
- ### part 2
	- $$v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{{x(3)-x(0.5)}}{3-0.5}=\frac{{21-3.5}}{2.5}=7 \frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$$
