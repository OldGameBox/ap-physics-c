## assignment
- Determine the [[center of mass]] of the uniform sheet of plywood whose total mass = $3\,\text{kg}$ (shown on the image).
- ![[Pasted image 20260224162250.png]]

## solution
- ### step 1
	- When solving such problems, we can treat each square as an object with mass that is concentrated at the [[center of mass|CoM]] of the square. 
	- ![[Pasted image 20260224162620.png]]
- ### step 2
	- Now that we have broken up the object into 3 squares, we can use their obvious [[center of mass|CoM]] to solve for the [[center of mass|CoM]] of the whole system.
	- ![[Pasted image 20260224162838.png]]
