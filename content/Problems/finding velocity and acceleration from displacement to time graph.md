## assignment
- ![[Pasted image 20260209211323.png]]
- Using given graph of [[displacement]] over time, find [[velocity]] and [[acceleration]] of an object.

## solution
- ### step 1
	- [[velocity|Velocity]] = slope of [[displacement]] over time.
	- $$v = \frac{dx}{dt} = \frac{{x-x_{0}}}{t-t_{0}} = \frac{{0.5\text{m}-0\text{m}}}{2\text{s}-0\text{s}}=0.25\,\text{m/s}$$
- ### step 2
	- [[acceleration|Acceleration]] = slope of [[velocity]] over time.
	- $$a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = 0\, \frac{m}{s^{2}}$$
