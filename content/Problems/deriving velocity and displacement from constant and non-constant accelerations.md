## assignment
- ### part 1
	- $\text{Constant Acceleration}\,(a) = 4$
	- Derive [[velocity]] and [[displacement]] from constant [[acceleration]].
- ### part 2
	- $\text{Acceleration}(a) = 4t -2$
	- Derive [[velocity]] and [[displacement]] from non-constant [[acceleration]].

## solution
- ### part 1
	- $$\begin{gather}a(t)=4 \\ \\v_{f} = \int a(t)\, {dt} = v_{i} + at \\v(t) = v_{i} + 4t \\ \\d=\int v(t)\, {dt} = v_{i}t + \frac{at^{2}}{2} + x_{0} \\d(x)=x_{0} +v_{i}t +\frac{4t^{2}}{2}\end{gather}$$
- ### part 2
	- $$\begin{gather}a=4t-2 \\ \\v_{f} = \int a(t) \, {dt} = \int(4t-2)\, dt \\v = 2t^2-2t + v_{i} \\ \\d=\int v(t)\, {dt} = \int(2t^2-2t + v_{i})dt \\d=\frac{2}{3}t^3-t^2+v_{i}t + x_{0}\end{gather}$$
