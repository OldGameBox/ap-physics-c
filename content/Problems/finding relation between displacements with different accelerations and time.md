## assignment
- A box is initially at rest. It the moves with a constant [[acceleration]], $a$. for a time, $t$, and ends with a final [[displacement]], $x_1$.
- The same box is again initially at rest. It then moves with a constant [[acceleration]], $\frac{1}{2}a$, for a time, $4t$, and ends with a final [[displacement]], $x_{2}$.
- How is $x_2$ related to $x_1$.

## solution
- ### step 1
	- **Determine which kinematics equation to apply.**
	- Since the problem mentions [[displacement]], [[velocity]], [[acceleration]], and time altogether, and the problem asks us to relate [[displacement|displacements]], we can use:
	- $$x=x_{0}+v_{0}t+\frac{1}{2}at^2$$
- ### step 2
	- **Derive an expression for the original scenario using only given variable and physical constants.**
	- $$x_{1}=\frac{1}{2}at^2$$
- ### step 3
	- **Derive an expression for the new scenario.**
	- $$x_{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{2} a\right)(4t)^2=4at^2$$
- ### step 4
	- **Relate**
	- $$x_{2}=8\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)at^2=8x_{1}$$
