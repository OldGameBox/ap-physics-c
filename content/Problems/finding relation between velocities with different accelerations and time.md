## assignment
- A box is initially at rest. It the moves with a constant [[acceleration]], $a$. for a time, $t$, and ends with a velocity, $v_1$.
- The same box is again initially at rest. It then moves with a constant [[acceleration]], $2a$, for a time, $\frac{1}{3}t$, and ends with a velocity, $v_{2}$.
- How is $v_2$ related to $v_1$

## solution
- ### step 1
	- **Determine which kinematics equation to apply.**
	- Since the problem only mentions [[velocity]], [[acceleration]], and time, and the problem asks us to relate [[velocity|velocities]], we can use:
	- $$v=v_{i}+at$$
- ### step 2
	- **Derive an expression for the original scenario using only given variable and physical constants.**
	- $$v_{1}=at$$
- ### step 3
	- **Derive an expression for the new scenario.**
	- $$v_{2}=\frac{2at}{3}$$
- ### step 4
	- **Relate**
	- $$v_{2} = \frac{2}{3}(at) = \frac{2}{3}v_{1}$$
