## assignment
- Let's have a disk with a mass of $1.8$ kg and radius of $0.4$ m rotating at rate $0.2$ rad/s. What is the disk's kinetic energy?

## solution
- ### step 1 - finding inertia
	- The formula for the inertia of $I = \frac{1}{2} mr^2$
	- $I = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1.8 \, \text{kg} \,\cdot\, (0.4 \text{m})^2 = 0.144 \,\text{kg m}^2$
- ### step 2 - finding kinetic energy
	- $$\begin{gather}K_{rot}=\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2 = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\,(0.144\,\text{kg m}^2)(0.2\,\text{rad/s})^2 = 0.00288\,\text{J}\end{gather}$$
