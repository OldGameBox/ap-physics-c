#u2 #forces 
- (*def.*) occurs when an object interacts with one of the following fields:
	- [[gravity|Gravity]], $F_g$
	- *Electric Force*, $F_E$
	- *Magnetic Force*,  $F_B$
- [[contact forces|Contact forces]], when analyzed on the microscopic level, are the **result of the electromagnetic forces**. The atoms within objects are bonded together via electrons.
