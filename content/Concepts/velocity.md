#u1 #kinematics 
- (*def.*) a [[vector|vector quantity]] that measures the rate at which an **object changes its position** ([[displacement]]) **over time**, including *both speed and direction.*
	- Unlike [[scalar]] *speed*, **velocity** requires **direction** and *can be zero if an object returns to its starting point*.
	- $$v=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$$
		- $v$ - **velocity**
		- $\Delta x$ - [[displacement]]
		- $\Delta t$ - *time*
