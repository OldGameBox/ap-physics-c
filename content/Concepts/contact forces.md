#u2 #forces
- (*def.*) occurs between two macroscopic objects that "touch" or come into contact with each other.
- *It is important to be able to identify forces that are acting on an object in various situations*
- ##### Examples:
	- Applied, $F_A$ (you push a book across a table)
	- Resistive Force, $F_r$ ([[friction]], $F_f$)
	- [[normal|Normal]], $F_N$ (support force of surface)
	- [[tension|Tension]], $F_T$ (from a string or rope)
