#u2 #forces
- (*def.*) an interaction between two objects.
	- **Forces** are [[vector|vectors]], and the direction of the net force on an object gives us information about how the object will [[acceleration|accelerate]].
	- **Force** on an object is always due to the interaction with another object. ***An object cannot exert a force on itself.***
- **Types of Forces**
	- [[contact forces]] - two objects *touch* each other
	- [[field force]] - objects interact through some field
