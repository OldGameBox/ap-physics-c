#u1 #kinematics
- (*def.*) **net change in object's position**, defined as the *straight-line distance* and *direction* from the *initial point* to the *final point*.
	- As a [[vector|vector quantity]], it includes both [[magnitude]] and **direction**, unlike [[scalar]] *distance*.
	- $$\Delta x=x_{f}-x_{i}$$
		- $\Delta x$ - **displacement**
		- $x_{f}$ - *final position*
		- $x_{i}$ - *initial position*
