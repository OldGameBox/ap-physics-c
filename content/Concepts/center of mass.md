#u2 #mass
- (*def.*) average position of the mass of a system of object.
- if the object is in a gravitational field, its **center of mass** is also known as *center of gravity*, because this location is the point where *gravity* can be considered to act on an object.
- The coordinates of the **center of mass** can be found mathematically:
	- $x_{cm}=\frac{{\sum m_{i}x_{i}}}{\sum m_{i}}$, where ^b369cf
		- $x_{cm}$ is the **x-position of the CoM**
		- $m_{i}$ is the **mass of each particle in the system**
		- $x_{i}$ is the **position of each particle in the system relative** to some point of origin that you select.
	- the same principle applies to other axes.
