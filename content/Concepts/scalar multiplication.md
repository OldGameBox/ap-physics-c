#u1 #vectors #multiplication
- (*def.*) multiplication of a [[vector|vector's]] [[magnitude]] by a [[scalar]] value, without changing its direction.
- When multiplying a *vector* in the [[vector#^506bfa|component form]], we must **multiply each component of axes** by the *scalar value*.
- $$\begin{aligned} \vec{a} & = 2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}+4\hat{k} \\ 4 \vec{a} & = 8\hat{i}+12\hat{j}+16\hat{k} \end{aligned}$$
