- ### lesson 2
	- Sometimes, [[force|forces]] are not directed along the $x$-axis or the $y$-axis. We must then analyze the components of those forces in those directions. 
		- If the forces in the $x$- direction are balanced, there is no [[acceleration]] of the object or system in the $x$-direction.
		- If the forces in the $y$-direction, there is no [[acceleration]] of the object or system in the $y$-direction.
		- *If object has no acceleration*, its **net force is 0**.
- ### lesson 3
	- In addition to analyzing each constituent particle of a system with a [[free-body diagrams|free-body diagram]], we can also consider a system as having all mass in a single point at the system's [[center of mass]] and analyze this point with a [[free-body diagrams|free-body diagram]].
