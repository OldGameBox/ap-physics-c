## notes
- ### lesson 1
	- ##### Problem-Solving Procedures:
		1. Identify the direction of the [[acceleration]].
			- *if the direction is not obvious, proceed to the next steps.*
		2. Draw [[free-body diagrams|free-body diagram(s)]] or [[force]] diagrams(s).
			- Resolve slanted vectors into components.
		3. Write $\Sigma F = F_{net}= ma$.
			- If in 2D, write $F_{net}$ for each dimension separately.
		4. Solve for what we need.
