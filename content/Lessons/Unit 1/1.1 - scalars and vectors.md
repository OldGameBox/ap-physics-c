## notes
- ### lesson 1
	- ##### Comparing Vectors, Scalars & Magnitudes

| Vectors      | Scalars                      | Magnitudes         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Displacement | Distance *(of displacement)* | $10\,m$            |
| Velocity     | Speed                        | $10\, \frac{m}{s}$ |
- ...
	- ...
		- The main difference between [[vector|vectors]] and [[scalar|scalars]] - *vectors* store *magnitude and direction*, while *scalars* only store *magnitude*.
	- ##### Notation
		- [[vector|Vectors]] can be noted in different ways
			- *look over* [[vector#^72ffbd|vector notations]]*.*
	- ##### Vector Magnitude and Angle
		- To find a [[magnitude]] of a [[vector]] we should use **Pythagorean Theorem**.
		- To find a **angle** of a [[vector]] we should use **arctangent** if we have have the *scalar values* for **each axis**.
		- [[finding a magnitude and an angle of a vector]]

	- ##### Component form addition & subtraction
		- When adding/subtracting **vectors** in *component form*, we just have to **add/subtract** **components of all corresponding axes**.
		- [[adding & subtracting vectors in component form]]
	- ##### Angle form addition & subtraction
		- When adding/subtracting **vectors** in *angle form*, first we must convert *angles and magnitudes* to **coordinates** using $\cos$ for $x$ and $sin$ for $y$. Then, we perform **regular component form addition/subtraction**. And, finally, calculate **angle and magnitude** of the *final vector*.
