- ### lesson 3
	- ##### The Calculus Relationship
		- $$\begin{gather} \text{Since }v=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}\implies v(t)=\frac{ds}{dt}=s'(t) , \\ \text{then } s(t)=s_{0}+\int^{t}_{t_{0}} v(u)\cdot du \text{ .}\end{gather}$$
		- $$\begin{gather} \text{Also since }a=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}\implies a(t)=\frac{dv}{dt}=v'(t) , \\ \text{then } v(t)=v_{0}+\int^{t}_{t_{0}} a(u)\cdot du \text{ .}\end{gather}$$
		- $$\begin{gather} \text{Hence, } s(t)=s_{0}+\int^{t}_{t_{0}} \left( v_{0}+\int^{t}_{t_{0}}a(u)du \right)d\tau\end{gather}$$
