- ### lesson 1

	- **What is Hulk's [[velocity]] with respect to Einstein, $v_{HE}$?**
		- $$v_{HE}=v_{HM}+v_{ME}$$
		- $v_{HE}$ - [[velocity]] of Hulk with respect to Einstein.
		- $v_{HM}$ - [[velocity]] of Hulk with respect to Minion.
		- $v_{ME}$ - [[velocity]] of Minion with respect to Einstein.
		- ![[Pasted image 20260210083535.png]]
		- $$\begin{gather}v_{HE}=-(v_{MH})+v_{ME} \\ v_{HE} = -0.3 - 0.2 = -0.5 \frac{m}{s} \end{gather}$$
