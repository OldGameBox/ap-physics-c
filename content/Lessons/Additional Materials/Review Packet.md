### Kinematics and Vectors

##### 1. Constant acceleration
$$v=v_0+at=0+(3.20)(6.00)=19.2\text{ m/s}$$

$$x=x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2=0+0+\frac{1}{2}(3.20)(6.00)^2=57.6\text{ m}$$

##### 2. Projectile through a window

$$y=y_0+x\tan\theta-\frac{gx^2}{2v_0^2\cos^2\theta}$$

$$y=1.20+(24.0)\tan(35^\circ)-\frac{9.8(24.0)^2}{2(18.0)^2\cos^2(35^\circ)}\approx5.02\text{ m}$$

##### 3. Relative velocity with wind


$$\vec{v}_{pg}=\left(\frac{90.0}{1.50}\right)\hat{j}=60.0\hat{j}\text{ km/h}$$

$$\vec{v}_{wg}=20.0\cos(30^\circ)\hat{i}+20.0\sin(30^\circ)\hat{j}=17.3\hat{i}+10.0\hat{j}\text{ km/h}$$

$$\vec{v}_{pa}=\vec{v}_{pg}-\vec{v}_{wg}=-17.3\hat{i}+50.0\hat{j}\text{ km/h}$$

$$|\vec{v}_{pa}|=\sqrt{(-17.3)^2+(50.0)^2}=52.9\text{ km/h, at }19.1^\circ\text{ W of N}$$

##### 4. Time-dependent acceleration


$$v(t)=\int a(t)dt=\int 0.60t\,dt=0.30t^2+2.00$$

$$v(4.00)=0.30(4.00)^2+2.00=6.80\text{ m/s}$$

$$x(t)=\int v(t)dt=\int (0.30t^2+2.00)dt=0.10t^3+2.00t$$

$$x(4.00)=0.10(4.00)^3+2.00(4.00)=14.4\text{ m}$$

### Forces and Momentum

##### 5. Time-dependent force


$$a(t)=\frac{F(t)}{m}=\frac{6.00+2.00t}{4.00}=1.50+0.50t$$

$$v(5.00)=\int_{0}^{5} (1.50+0.50t)dt=\left[1.50t+0.25t^2\right]_0^5=13.75\text{ m/s}$$

$$x(5.00)=\int_{0}^{5} (1.50t+0.25t^2)dt=\left[0.75t^2+\frac{0.25}{3}t^3\right]_0^5=29.17\text{ m}$$

##### 6. Time-dependent friction


$$a(t)=-\mu_k(t)g=-(0.100+0.0200t)(9.8)=-0.98-0.196t$$

$$v(t)=v_0+\int a(t)dt=12.0-0.98t-0.098t^2=0\implies t=7.14\text{ s}$$

$$x=\int_{0}^{7.14} (12.0-0.98t-0.098t^2)dt=\left[12.0t-0.49t^2-\frac{0.098}{3}t^3\right]_0^{7.14}=48.8\text{ m}$$

### Forces, Work, and Energy

##### 7. Position-dependent force

$$W=\int_{0}^{4} (20.0-3.00x)dx=\left[20.0x-1.50x^2\right]_0^4=80.0-24.0=56.0\text{ J}$$

$$\Delta K=W\implies\frac{1}{2}mv_f^2-\frac{1}{2}mv_i^2=56.0$$

$$\frac{1}{2}(2.00)v_f^2-\frac{1}{2}(2.00)(2.00)^2=56.0\implies v_f=\sqrt{60.0}=7.75\text{ m/s}$$

##### 8. Acceleration as a function of position

$$v\,dv=a(x)dx\implies\int_{3.00}^{v_f} v\,dv=\int_{0}^{6.00} 0.500x\,dx$$

$$\left[\frac{1}{2}v^2\right]_{3.00}^{v_f}=\left[0.250x^2\right]_0^{6.00}\implies\frac{1}{2}v_f^2-4.50=9.00$$

$$v_f^2=27.0\implies v_f=5.20\text{ m/s}$$

### Impulse and Momentum

##### 9. Impulse from a force-time graph


$$J=\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}(4.00\text{ s})(24.0\text{ N})=48.0\text{ N}\cdot\text{s}$$

$$J=m(v_f-v_i)\implies 48.0=3.00(v_f-1.50)$$

$$v_f=1.50+16.0=17.5\text{ m/s}$$

##### 10. One-dimensional collision


$$m_1v_{1i}+m_2v_{2i}=m_1v_{1f}+m_2v_{2f}$$

$$(2.00)(6.00)+(4.00)(-1.00)=2.00v_{1f}+(4.00)(2.50)\implies v_{1f}=-1.00\text{ m/s}$$

$$K_i=\frac{1}{2}(2.00)(6.00)^2+\frac{1}{2}(4.00)(-1.00)^2=38.0\text{ J}$$

$$K_f=\frac{1}{2}(2.00)(-1.00)^2+\frac{1}{2}(4.00)(2.50)^2=13.5\text{ J}$$

$$\%\text{ Lost}=\frac{38.0-13.5}{38.0}\times 100=64.5\%$$

### Oscillations and Energy

##### 11. Nonlinear spring work


$$K_i=\frac{1}{2}(1.50)(5.00)^2=18.75\text{ J}$$

$$W_s=\int_{0}^{x_{max}} (200x+40x^2)dx=100x_{max}^2+\frac{40}{3}x_{max}^3$$

$$100x_{max}^2+13.33x_{max}^3=18.75\implies x_{max}=0.413\text{ m}$$

##### 12. Pendulum energy


$$h=L(1-\cos\theta)=1.50(1-\cos 25^\circ)=0.1405\text{ m}$$

$$v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{2(9.8)(0.1405)}=1.66\text{ m/s}$$

$$T-mg=\frac{mv^2}{L}\implies T=(1.20)(9.8)+\frac{1.20(1.66)^2}{1.50}=14.0\text{ N}$$

##### 13. Simple harmonic motion


$$\omega=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}=\sqrt{\frac{100}{0.250}}=20.0\text{ rad/s}$$

$$T=\frac{2\pi}{\omega}=\frac{2\pi}{20.0}=0.314\text{ s}$$

$$v_{max}=A\omega=(0.0800)(20.0)=1.60\text{ m/s}$$

$$a_{max}=A\omega^2=(0.0800)(20.0)^2=32.0\text{ m/s}^2$$

##### 14. Linear drag after launch

$$v(t)=v_0e^{-bt/m}=20.0e^{-4.00(3.00)/2.00}=20.0e^{-6.00}=0.0496\text{ m/s}$$

$$x(t)=\int_{0}^{3.00} v(t)dt=\frac{mv_0}{b}\left(1-e^{-bt/m}\right)=\frac{(2.00)(20.0)}{4.00}\left(1-e^{-6.00}\right)=9.98\text{ m}$$

##### 15. Variable friction stopping distance


$$W_{nc}=\int_{0}^{x_{stop}} -\mu_k(x)mg\,dx=-mg\left[0.150x+0.0100x^2\right]_0^{x_{stop}}$$

$$\Delta K=0-\frac{1}{2}(5.00)(10.0)^2=-250\text{ J}$$

$$-(5.00)(9.8)(0.150x_{stop}+0.0100x_{stop}^2)=-250$$

$$0.490x_{stop}^2+7.35x_{stop}-250=0\implies x_{stop}=16.3\text{ m}$$

### Rotational Dynamics

##### 16. Constant torque


$$\alpha=\frac{\tau}{I}=\frac{3.00}{0.750}=4.00\text{ rad/s}^2$$

$$\omega=\alpha t=(4.00)(4.00)=16.0\text{ rad/s}$$

$$\theta=\frac{1}{2}\alpha t^2=\frac{1}{2}(4.00)(4.00)^2=32.0\text{ rad}$$

##### 17. Time-dependent torque


$$\alpha(t)=\frac{\tau(t)}{I}=\frac{4.00t}{2.00}=2.00t\text{ rad/s}^2$$

$$\omega(6.00)=\omega_0+\int_{0}^{6.00} 2.00t\,dt=1.00+\left[t^2\right]_0^{6.00}=37.0\text{ rad/s}$$

$$\theta(6.00)=\int_{0}^{6.00} (1.00+t^2)dt=\left[t+\frac{t^3}{3}\right]_0^{6.00}=6.00+\frac{216}{3}=78.0\text{ rad}$$

##### 18. Nonuniform rod


$$M=\int_{0}^{3.00} \lambda(x)dx=\int_{0}^{3.00} 2.00x\,dx=\left[x^2\right]_0^{3.00}=9.00\text{ kg}$$

$$I=\int_{0}^{3.00} x^2\lambda(x)dx=\int_{0}^{3.00} 2.00x^3\,dx=\left[\frac{1}{2}x^4\right]_0^{3.00}=40.5\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$$

$$\alpha=\frac{\tau}{I}=\frac{rF\sin\theta}{I}=\frac{3.00(18.0)\sin(30^\circ)}{40.5}=0.667\text{ rad/s}^2$$

##### 19. Angular work from variable torque


$$W=\int_{0}^{3.00} \tau(\theta)d\theta=\int_{0}^{3.00} (5.00+2.00\theta)d\theta=\left[5.00\theta+\theta^2\right]_0^{3.00}=24.0\text{ J}$$

$$\frac{1}{2}I\omega_f^2=W\implies\frac{1}{2}(0.800)\omega_f^2=24.0\implies\omega_f=7.75\text{ rad/s}$$

##### 20. Exponential torque pulse


$$\tau(t)=rF(t)=0.250(40.0e^{-0.200t})=10.0e^{-0.200t}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$$

$$\Delta L=I\omega_f=\int_{0}^{5.00} 10.0e^{-0.200t}dt=\left[\frac{10.0}{-0.200}e^{-0.200t}\right]_0^{5.00}=-50.0(e^{-1.00}-1)=31.6\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}$$

$$\omega_f=\frac{31.6}{0.500}=63.2\text{ rad/s}$$

### Fluids

##### 21. Fluids: Buoyancy


$$\text{Fraction submerged}=\frac{\rho_{obj}}{\rho_f}=\frac{700}{1000}=0.700$$

$$F_B=\rho_f V_{sub}g=(1000)(0.700\times 0.0200)(9.8)=137\text{ N}$$

##### 22. Continuity and Bernoulli


$$A_1v_1=A_2v_2\implies v_2=v_1\left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2=6.00\left(\frac{2.00}{5.00}\right)^2=0.960\text{ m/s}$$

$$P_1+\frac{1}{2}\rho v_1^2=P_2+\frac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2$$

$$P_2=1.20\times 10^5+\frac{1}{2}(1000)(6.00^2-0.960^2)=1.38\times 10^5\text{ Pa}$$

##### 23. Open manometer


$$P_{gas}=P_{atm}+\rho g \Delta h$$

$$P_{gas}=1.013\times 10^5+(1050)(9.8)(0.250)=1.039\times 10^5\text{ Pa}$$

### Thermal Physics

##### 25. Heating with phase change

$$t=\frac{Q}{P}=\frac{109680}{500}=219\text{ s}$$

##### 26. Expansion from a temperature gradient

$$\Delta L=(1.80\times 10^{-4})\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^{2.00}=3.60\times 10^{-4}\text{ m}$$

##### 27. Conduction and entropy rate


$$P=\frac{kA\Delta T}{d}=\frac{0.0400(0.500)(295-273)}{0.0300}=14.7\text{ W}$$

$$\frac{dS_{tot}}{dt}=\frac{P}{T_c}-\frac{P}{T_h}=\frac{14.67}{273}-\frac{14.67}{295}=4.01\times 10^{-3}\text{ W/K}$$
