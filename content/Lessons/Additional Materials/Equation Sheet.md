### Kinematics
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta v = \frac{dx}{dt} & \qquad a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}\\ \\
v_{f}=v_{i}+at & \qquad \Delta x=v_{i}t+\frac{1}{2}at^2 \\
v_{f}^2=v_{i}^2+2a\Delta x & \qquad \Delta x = \frac{1}{2}(v_{f}+v_{i})\Delta t
\end{aligned}$$
### Rotational Kinematics
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta x=r\Delta\theta & \qquad v_{trans} =r\omega & a_{trans}=r\alpha & \qquad a_{cent.p.} =\frac{{v^2}}{r}=\omega^2r \\
\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}
r - \text{radius} & \qquad \omega-\text{ang vel} & \alpha-\text{ang acc}
\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\omega=\frac{d\theta}{dt} & \qquad \alpha=\frac{d\omega}{dt}=\frac{{d^2\theta}}{dt^2} \\
\omega_{f} = \omega_{i}+\alpha t & \qquad \Delta\theta=\omega_{i}t + \frac{1}{2}\alpha t^2 \\
\omega_{f}^2=\omega_{i}^2+2\alpha \Delta\theta & \qquad \Delta\theta =\frac{1}{2}(\omega_{f} + \omega_{i})t 
\end{aligned}$$
