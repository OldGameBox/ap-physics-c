## Part B
Write the translational equation of motion for the block.

$$M g \sin(\phi) - T = M a$$

Write the rotational equation of motion for the pulley.

$$T R = I \alpha$$

Substitute the given rotational inertia $I = \frac{1}{3} M R^2$ and angular acceleration $\alpha = \frac{a}{R}$.

$$T R = \left(\frac{1}{3} M R^2\right) \left(\frac{a}{R}\right)$$

Solve for tension $T$.

$$T = \frac{1}{3} M a$$

Substitute tension $T$ into the block equation.

$$M g \sin(\phi) - \frac{1}{3} M a = M a$$

Combine the acceleration terms.

$$M g \sin(\phi) = \frac{4}{3} M a$$

Solve for linear acceleration $a$.

$$a = \frac{3}{4} g \sin(\phi)$$

## Part D
#### Tension Comparison
The new tension $T_{new}$ is less than the original tension $T_{0}$.
#### Justification
The addition of friction on the incline opposes the downward motion of the block. The net force on the block decreases. A smaller net force causes a smaller linear acceleration for the system. The rotational equation for the pulley dictates $T = \frac{1}{3} M a$. Tension is directly proportional to linear acceleration. The smaller acceleration directly results in a smaller tension force $T_{new}$.
